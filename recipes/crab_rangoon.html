Crab Rangoon

Description

Crispy wontons filled with creamy crab and chives.

Back to Home

Ingredients

  • 8 ounces softened cream cheese
  • 3 tablespoons sliced chives
  • 1 teaspoon low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 6 ounces lump crab meat
  • 1 egg
  • 25 wonton wrappers
  • Neutral frying oil

Instructions

  1. Combine cheese, chives, soy sauce, and garlic powder. Gently incorporate crab.
  2. Beat egg. Fill each wrapper with a tablespoon of filling. Brush edges with egg and seal corners together.
  3. Chill for one hour.
  4. Fry small batches at 350°F for 3 to 4 minutes until golden. Drain and serve.
