Garlic Butter Shrimp

Description

Shrimp cooked in a buttery garlic sauce with lemon and parsley. Serve it with rice, pasta, or as an appetizer!

Back to Home

Ingredients

  • 4 tablespoons butter
  • 1 pound large shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • 2–3 teaspoons minced garlic
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 1 tablespoon chopped parsley

Instructions

  1. Melt butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. Add shrimp and sprinkle with salt, pepper, and Italian seasoning.
  3. Stir occasionally while cooking for 3–5 minutes, until the shrimp turn pink and opaque.
  4. Mix in the minced garlic and cook for another minute.
  5. Add lemon juice and parsley, stir everything together, and serve.
